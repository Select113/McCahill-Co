Crew Schedule & Replacement Hours — June 2026

Every site visit in June, who was on it and for how long, and which employee hours replaced Kyle in the field.

How employee hours are treated.
  • Kyle on site with the crew → the employee's hours are payroll cost and are deducted from labour revenue. Kyle's own hours are never deducted.
  • Employee on a site without Kyle, or still on site after Kyle has left → those are replacement hours. They can't be deducted, so they are listed below and removed from the payroll cost.
  • Wage rates: Kierran $27.00/hr · Booker $30.00/hr. Only Kierran's and Booker's hours are tallied here.
