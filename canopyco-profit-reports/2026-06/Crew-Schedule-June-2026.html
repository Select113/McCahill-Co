Crew Schedule & Replacement Hours — June 2026

Every site visit in June, who was on it and for how long, and which employee hours replaced Kyle in the field.

How employee hours are treated.
  • Employee hours worked while Kyle is working, on the same site or a different one, are payroll cost and are deducted from labour revenue. Kyle's own hours are never deducted.
  • Employee hours worked while Kyle is not working are replacement hours: the employee is filling in for Kyle. They can't be deducted, so they are listed below and removed from the payroll cost.
  • Example: Kyle works 8:00–12:00 with Kierran while Booker works 8:00–2:00 elsewhere. Only Booker's 12:00–2:00 (2 hours) are replacement hours.
  • Wage rates: Kierran $27.00/hr · Booker $30.00/hr. Only Kierran's and Booker's hours are tallied here.
