## 4. What cannot be computed

**Job profit is not determinable from these files.** The missing input is labour cost:

- 292.5 corrected on-site crew hours were billed at $50/hr, but crew **wage cost per hour
  is not recorded anywhere** in this repository.
- The split-rail fence was a fixed $5,450 supply-and-install. Its two days of installation
  labour appear **nowhere in the daily labour log** — so those hours are a real cost with no
  matching hour record.
- Owner time (sourcing, pickups, deliveries, design) is deliberately unbilled and unlogged.

Contribution after documented materials is $46,090.08 − $16,348.70 = **$29,741.38**, which
must still absorb all crew wages, the fence install labour, vehicle/fuel, insurance, and
overhead. That is a contribution figure, **not profit**.
