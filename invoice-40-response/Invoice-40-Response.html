F. Materials Markup / Handling

Materials and plants carry a standard markup. That markup is what covers the sourcing, procurement, pickup, delivery, and handling of materials and plantings — work that, as noted in Section B, is not billed to you separately as labour or travel. This is standard practice for landscape supply-and-install work, and on the larger hard-goods lines (stone, aggregate, excavation) the effective markup is modest.

The underlying supplier receipts and invoices are retained in full and are available for your inspection on request if you would like to review the supply costs behind any line.
