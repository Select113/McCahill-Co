Pricing method: materials and plants are priced at roughly double supplier cost (a ~100% markup) as a rule of thumb. That markup is what covers all sourcing, hand-selection, pickups, deliveries, and handling — none of which is billed separately as labour or travel time. In practice it runs lower on large bulk orders (the main K2 stone order was ~40%) and higher on small-quantity pickups. Across the whole project the blended markup is about 70%.

† Excavation was performed by a subcontractor (G&C); the figure includes on-site coordination and supervision.
‡ Cost is taken from our records; the supplier receipt copy is still being located and will be forwarded if found.
§ Small-quantity pickup — the price reflects a dedicated sourcing/pickup trip (loading and transport), which is not billed as a separate labour or travel line.

Minor consumables (geotextile fabric, fabric pins, weed spray) were genuine project costs but receipts were not retained; these are small relative to the totals above. The split-rail fence is a separate fixed-price supply-and-install item and is addressed in the covering email — it is not a cost-plus material line and is therefore excluded from the figures above.
