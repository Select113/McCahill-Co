MARKUP METHOD
My rule of thumb is to roughly double supplier cost — about a 100% markup — on materials and plants.
That markup is what covers all the sourcing, hand-selection, pickups, deliveries, and handling, none
of which I bill separately as labour or travel. In practice it runs lower than that on large bulk
orders (the main K2 stone order, for example, worked out to about 40%) and higher on small-quantity
pickups, where a low-cost material still needs a full trip to source and collect. Across the whole
project, the blended markup comes to about 70%, as the schedule shows.
